About me

When I was little, my dad worked in IT at Alexion (a pharmaceutical company based in Cheshire, CT at the time) back before it became the giant it is now. One bring-your-child-to-work day, they had the kids extract DNA from a strawberry. I don't remember much from being that young, but I remember that.

It stuck with me in a way that's hard to explain. I ended up at BU studying biomedical engineering, which feels like the long answer to why. I like problems that sit at the edge of biology and design.

Outside of engineering, I write fiction, go on hikes, and spend probably too much time in open world video games. I grew up in a small town in Connecticut, about an hour outside of New York.
